# Independent root review of the final 10 Hz speech replies

Root read all 60 actual selected-CE6775 replies, including each controlled history
arm and each four-turn rollout. This is an unblinded model-assisted diagnostic;
the previous baseline review was known. No response or metric was edited.

The six retained-audio controlled next-step replies and all six rollout
next-step replies contain no concrete scenario-specific action. Promises to
find, clarify or focus on a next step are not the requested next step. The six
current-audio/text-history controls likewise fail this action criterion.

All retained-audio controlled endings keep discussing or moving forward rather
than explicitly wrapping up. Four of six current-audio/text-history endings
say "We can wrap up here"; the spreadsheet pair instead proposes moving
forward with a next step. Thus this closer-to-training format now demonstrates
some closure understanding, unlike the parent, while retaining the action
failure. Every omit-initial ending invents inability to end a conversation or
stop asking questions, illustrating misleading fluent refusal.

Five rollout endings plainly continue assistance. The happy draft ending says
"we can proceed without further discussion"; it is borderline closure because
it simultaneously interprets the request as moving forward. Under the earlier
conservative requirement for explicit wrap-up, root would not count it as a
clear success. This sensitivity is documented rather than concealed.

Some initial draft and cafe responses change warmth between intended happy/sad
deliveries, but later replies lose specifics and become generic. The angry
spreadsheet opening sounds happy, and the sad opening is also upbeat. Subsequent
differences do not demonstrate useful delayed emotional memory. These six cases
from three literal scenarios cannot establish a general dialogue accuracy rate.

The separately hash-bound merge retains exact text, ASR and initial-tone
references; the evaluator's full matched review supplies their case-level
comparison. Frozen text weights, current utterance injection and fresh-prefill
behavior remain distinct from the quality observed here.
